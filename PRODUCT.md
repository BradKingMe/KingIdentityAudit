# KingIdentity Audit

Local identity evidence workbench for identity administrators and IT operators reviewing AD DS, Entra ID and Intune evidence. Users investigate access, understand recorded posture and activity, compare snapshots and prepare owner-led reviews.

The existing product and approved expansion define this brief: extend Access with evidence-backed membership paths, direct active administrative/application assignments, employee review cases, and exportable action plans. Preserve the Fluent UI/Segoe operational interface. Default to clearly labeled synthetic evidence; live collection is optional, delegated and read-only.

Success means an operator can select a person, understand recorded assignments and missing coverage, capture a review, assign an owner, record decisions and export a restorable evidence package. It does not establish effective authorization, HR status, security certification or execute remediation. Data stays in browser memory until explicitly exported; real exports are sensitive.
